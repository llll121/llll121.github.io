I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Data Science, Analytics and Engineering at [Arizona State University](https://www.asu.edu/), advised by [Prof. Xiyang Hu](https://xiyanghu.github.io/). My research focuses on **LLM-based agentic systems**, **AI safety**, and **trustworthy machine learning**. I am especially interested in how LLM agents behave when their tools, skills, or collaborators change, and in evaluation methods that surface risks that aggregate metrics miss.

My recent work measures how communication topology shapes private-memory leakage in multi-agent LLM systems ([Findings of ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1980/)), and audits how agent skills reshape agent behavior even when pass rates barely move ([ICML 2026 FAGEN Workshop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11946)).

Before ASU, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from the [University of Southern California](https://www.usc.edu/), where I worked with [Prof. Yan Liu](https://melady.usc.edu/) on time series foundation models and LLM reasoning over time series, and with [Prof. Yue Zhao](https://viterbi-web.usc.edu/~yzhao010/) on privacy in multi-agent LLM systems. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Troy University and my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Jiangsu University of Technology through a joint program.
